What counts as evidence?

Source history shows what was implemented. Tests check specific behavior. A deployment receipt identifies a released version. Owner acceptance records the observed result or an explicit decision. One does not substitute for the others.

Example — Smart Sync: I required investigation of a receipt mismatch without duplicate contacts or premature clearing of failures. Agent-delivered code corrected a missing field alias; deployment and an owner-reported successful test are recorded separately. See selected work.
