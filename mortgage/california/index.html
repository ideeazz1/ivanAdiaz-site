01 / California option

CalHFA

California Housing Finance Agency programs offer first-mortgage options for eligible homebuyers. The first mortgage and the assistance are separate parts of the financing.

  • Who it fits

    Buyers purchasing a primary residence who meet the selected program’s borrower and property requirements.

  • How it works

    CalHFA offers government and conventional first-mortgage programs. Compatible assistance depends on the program.

  • What to check

    Income limits, credit requirements, homebuyer education and lender participation before selecting a loan.

Decide with: your monthly budget, available savings, the property and the requirements of the selected loan.

02 / California option

MyHome

MyHome is a deferred-payment junior loan for eligible first-time buyers using a CalHFA first mortgage. It can help with down payment and closing costs.

  • Who it fits

    Eligible first-time buyers who will live in the home, meet income limits and complete required homebuyer education.

  • How the help works

    Payments on the assistance loan are deferred. The loan is still a debt; deferred does not mean forgiven.

  • What to check

    The assistance amount depends on the first-mortgage type. Review when repayment becomes due.

Decide with: the cash needed at closing, the combined loan structure, and what happens if you sell, refinance or pay off the first mortgage.

03 / California option

Dream For All

Dream For All combines a qualifying first mortgage with a shared-appreciation assistance loan. Repayment includes the original assistance plus a share of the home’s appreciation.

  • Who it fits

    First-time buyers who meet CalHFA’s requirements, including a qualifying first-generation buyer and California residency criteria.

  • How the help works

    The program uses a voucher selection process. Eligibility alone does not reserve funding or guarantee selection.

  • Availability check

    Check CalHFA’s current application window and voucher updates. This page is not an invitation to an open funding round.

Decide with: eligibility, voucher availability and the future repayment obligation before treating this as a purchase plan.
